REVERSE Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how REVERSE ("the App") collects, uses, and shares information from users.

Information We Collect

REVERSE collects minimal information to provide its core functionality:

  • Local data: Tasks, goals, and settings you create are stored locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit this data.
  • App settings: Language preference, sound/vibration preferences are stored locally.

Advertising

REVERSE uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device information including:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device type and operating system
  • IP address
  • App usage data for advertising purposes

For more information about Google's privacy practices, see Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties. The only data shared is anonymous data with Google AdMob for advertising purposes.

Children's Privacy

REVERSE is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Reset your data by uninstalling the app
  • Disable personalized ads in your device settings
  • Contact us with any privacy concerns

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
